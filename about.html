Our Story

Coding kitchen started in 2023 inside Ile-Ife. We noticed students in OAU and people in Lagere, Mayfair, Parakin didn't have a place to get tasty burgers, crispy chicken and cold drinks at affordable price with fast delivery.

So we started small - one kitchen, two staffs. Today we serve over 500 customers monthly. Everything still fresh, still affordable.

We cook with clean oil, fresh chicken, and we don't store food for next day. If it's not fresh, we don't sell it.
